01 / ABOUT

Medical physics at the intersection of physics, computation and patient care.

Your photograph

I am a Medical Physicist working in Radiation Oncology, with interests spanning clinical radiotherapy, dosimetry, treatment-planning systems, radiation transport and computational medical physics.

My research interests include Monte Carlo simulation, deterministic transport methods, radiotherapy dose calculation and AI/ML applications in medical physics.

I am particularly interested in connecting rigorous physical models with practical clinical problems.
